# LIB Pack

Every mod in this repo in **one jar**, for Minecraft **26.3** (Fabric):

| Mod | What it adds |
|---|---|
| [Market](../README.md) | Sell anything for Marks (₥), buy items back, flex with pallets of cash |
| [Colonycraft](../colonycraft/README.md) | Colonies, prefab buildings and villager workers paid in Marks: farms, ranches, apiaries, fisheries, tobacco farms, a harbor with a Loading Dock, a train station, medieval walls and towers, and a town street with a trading post (master traders), a bank with a shared walk-in vault, a museum for your relics, a chapel, a library and a fuel depot, plus a guildhouse that hires Sellswords cheaper, and a distillery and a tavern that turn your grain into genever money. A Headquarters runs them all as one network: every colony on one table, convoys between their storehouses, optional roles, and mayors who rehire on their own. Storehouses are real warehouses |
| [Ahoy](../ahoy/README.md) | A sailing ship in a bottle: captain + 8 passengers, cargo holds, wind |
| [Burlap Sack](../burlap-sack/README.md) | Bag villagers and wandering traders, let them out somewhere else |
| [Cannon](../cannon/README.md) | An aimable cannon and iron + gunpowder cannonballs |
| [Cargo Train](../cargo-train/README.md) | A self-driving locomotive and cargo wagons that haul between station chests |
| [Flintlock](../flintlock/README.md) | Flintlock pistols, muskets and blunderbusses, with cartridges and slow reloads |
| [Havana](../havana/README.md) | Grow tobacco, cure it in a barrel, roll and smoke cigars |
| [Warehouse](../warehouse/README.md) | Big sorted storage: a core plus racks, intake chests, train drop-off, Loading Docks for ships, drill rigs unloading straight in, and networks of branches around a central warehouse |
| [Skills](../skills/README.md) | Elder Scrolls style skills: level up by doing, spend perk points every 10 levels |
| [Hamlets & Horrors](../hamlets/README.md) | Random cottages, castles and dungeons: some lived in by villagers, some overrun by monsters |
| [Overenchant](../overenchant/README.md) | Higher maximum enchantment levels: every enchantment goes to X (Sharpness X instead of V). Flintlock guns can be enchanted too |
| [Fuck Illagers](../fuck-illagers/README.md) | Bounty hunting: illager fingers for Marks, and contracts on named illager bosses in wagons, towers, camps, fortresses, dungeons and castles |
| [Fossil Fool](../fossil-fool/README.md) | Old-timey oil: dowse for pockets, sink a 5×5 shaft with a fuel-guzzling Drill Rig, strike crude, tank it (or water, or lava), pipe it across the map (or from an offshore rig), upgrade the rig in its workshop, refine it into diesel and burn it in an Industrial Oven that smelts ores double |
| [Riches](../riches/README.md) | Show off: a walk-in vault where your Market balance piles up in gold, locked vault doors, display cases with plaques, and 48 one-of-a-kind relics |
| [Blimey](../blimey/README.md) | A riveted iron airship that runs on Fossil Fool diesel: refits for speed, fuel economy and cargo, plus small, big and huge bombs that wreck buildings |
| [Sellswords](../sellswords/README.md) | Hire Dutch mercenaries with crossbows and swords: they follow you, hold a spot, hunt with you, guard your walls and villagers, board your ships and airships, and climb the ranks for gold to Musketeer or Foestopper Bulwark |
| [Proost](../proost/README.md) | Ferment wheat, sugar cane and apples into mash, distill genever, whisky, rum and brandy in a cauldron over a fire, age them in oak casks, and drink until the world spins (Dutch Courage included) |
| [De Compagnie](../compagnie/README.md) | Run a shady VOC trading company from a Company Desk: hire agents, bankroll 36 trade, raid, explore and smuggling jobs three ways, answer telegrams from the road, lend money to dubious borrowers and send collectors after them, grow from a Charter to the VOC |
| [De Hooghe Heeren](../hooghe-heeren/README.md) | Optional rival houses behind a tab on the Company Desk (each player chooses to take a seat): lords with treasuries, regiments and long memories who play by your rules. Gifts, pacts, alliances and tribute; spies, saboteurs, forged letters and assassins run by your agents; wars with pitched battles at noon you can ride out to and fight in; seats in the world to visit (or storm), and an Audience Bell hall to feast your guests in (or bar the doors) |
| [Tulpengekte](../tulpengekte/README.md) | Tulip mania, balanced: plant bulbs, breed rare broken varieties (1 in 1,000 is the Semper Augustus), and sell them to a Bloemist who wants three varieties a day |
| [Specerij](../specerij/README.md) | Spices that only grow where they grow: pepper, nutmeg, cloves, cinnamon, tea and saffron in their home biomes, a glass-roofed Hortus to cheat the climate at home, a Drying Rack, and Colonycraft Spice Plantations that pay more the farther from home they are |
| [Terra Incognita](../terra-incognita/README.md) | A new dimension, reached by sea with a chart: an archipelago of jungle, savanna, desert and badlands islands with mountains rising out of a warm sea, everything the Overworld spawns (and more), crawling with illagers, every chest stuffed with extra plunder |

It's **server-side only**, like the mods inside it. Friends join with a plain vanilla client.

## New in 2.3.1: sail your own ship to Terra Incognita

- **Terra Incognita 1.1 + Ahoy 1.8**: set a course from the deck of your Ahoy ship and the ship makes the crossing too, with her holds, refits, guns and bunks, and the whole crew back in their seats on the other side. Fill the holds with plunder and sail her home. The hull needs **Copper sheathing** (rigging II) from the Shipwright first.

## New in 2.3: Terra Incognita

- **[Terra Incognita](../terra-incognita/README.md)**, mod number 23: a whole new dimension. Craft a **Sea Chart of the Southland**, take it to sea and hold your course: the fog takes you and your crew to an archipelago of jungle, savanna, desert and badlands islands with mountains rising out of a warm sea. Everything the Overworld spawns spawns there too, your structure mods included. It's crawling with illagers, and every unopened chest holds extra plunder. High risk, high reward.
- **Cannon**: cannonball block damage is a switch now. Ops run `/cannon blockdamage on` (or `off`); it's off by default.
- **Colonycraft**: the Town Hall has two tabs, **Buy blueprints** (by kind) and **Your buildings**.

## New in 2.2: Specerij

- **[Specerij](../specerij/README.md)**, mod number 22: six spices that only grow in their home biomes (jungle pepper, mangrove nutmeg, bamboo-jungle cloves, savanna cinnamon, cherry-grove tea, meadow saffron). Bring a cutting home and grow it in a **Hortus** (a glass roof and a fire going) at half speed, then dry it in a **Drying Rack**.
- **Colonycraft Spice Plantations**: only where spices grow wild, and they bring in more the farther the colony is from your Headquarters (up to double at 5,000 blocks). Faraway colonies finally pay.

## New in 2.1: Tulpengekte

- **[Tulpengekte](../tulpengekte/README.md)**, mod number 21: plant tulip bulbs, lift them for offsets, and hope one breaks into a Viceroy or the Semper Augustus. A **Bloemist** villager buys three varieties a day at +50%. Colonycraft farms dig up the odd bulb.
- **Riches** has **48 relics** in 8 collections now: The Golden Age, The Frozen North, Curiosities of the End and Wonders of the Wild join the original four.

## New in 2.0: one realm

The mods play together:

- **Govern from home.** A **Governor's Desk** (₥750 at any Town Hall) can take the charter (₥6,000), and then your own house is your Headquarters. The colony it stands in is your capital.
- **One treasury.** The Governor's Table counts everything you have: your balance, your banks' vaults, Compagnie stakes, loans and strongbox. It shows a day's colony books next to Compagnie wages and the Heeren's upkeep, pacts and tribute, and adds up tomorrow's bills from all of it.
- **Rivals at your gates.** The Heeren's riders raid your **convoys**, and at war their armies **besiege** your colonies: no work and no convoys until you win the battle at the walls or make peace. The new **Fortress** role holds out.
- **Sellswords on the field.** Your mercenaries fight the Heeren's soldiers in pitched battles and seat garrisons.
- **Shield**, the 24th skill: block hits to learn it; bash, riposte and catch your breath.
- **Statecraft**, the 23rd skill: pacts, errands, feasts and victories make you a better ruler.

## Install

1. Install [Fabric Loader](https://fabricmc.net/use/server/) for Minecraft 26.3.
2. Put **Fabric API** and **`lib-pack-<version>.jar`** in the server's `mods/` folder.
3. **Remove the separate jars** of these mods if you had them installed. Your worlds, balances, colonies and config files carry over: they belong to the mods, not the jar.

Get the jar from the [lib-pack-latest release](https://github.com/ThatCoffeelock/Market/releases/tag/lib-pack-latest).

## How it's built

The pack doesn't contain any code of its own. It nests the jars that the other projects build (Fabric's "jar-in-jar"), and Fabric Loader loads them as if they were in `mods/` separately. Each mod is still released on its own too.

To build it yourself (Java 25): run `./gradlew build` in the repo root and in each mod folder, then `./gradlew build` in `lib-pack/`. The jar ends up in `lib-pack/build/libs/`.

There's a printable tutorial with one page per mod in `tutorial/LIB-Pack-Tutorial.pdf` (rebuild it with `python3 tutorial/build.py`). There is also a one-page player guide with every recipe, control and command: open `field-guide.html` in a browser.

CI (`.github/workflows/lib-pack.yml`) builds all twenty-three mods and the pack, then installs the pack on a real Fabric server, the way a server owner would, and checks that all twenty-three mods load and the server starts.
