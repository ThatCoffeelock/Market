Minecraft 26.3 · Fabric · server-side

LIB Pack

Thirteen mods in one jar. Run an economy in Marks (₥), found colonies, sail ships, haul cargo by train, stack it all in warehouses, drive camper vans and tanks, fire cannons and muskets, kidnap villagers, smoke cigars, level 13 skills, raid castles that were already there and push every enchantment to level X. Players join with a plain vanilla client.

Install

  1. Install Fabric Loader for 26.3 on the server.
  2. Put Fabric API and lib-pack-1.6.0+mc26.3.jar in mods/.
  3. Remove the separate mod jars if you had them. Worlds, balances and colonies carry over.

Recipes unlock in the normal recipe book. Ops get any item with each mod's give command.

Havana · from seed to stogie

The tobacco road

  1. 1Break grass and ferns for Tobacco Seeds1 in 12
  2. 2Plant on farmland. Bone meal workslike any crop
  3. 3Ripe plants shoot up 2 blocks. Harvest 3–5 leaves+1–2 seeds
  4. 4Leaves in a Curing Barrel become Cured Tobacco1 day · 20 min
  5. 5Leave it in for Aged Tobacco (optional)+2 days
  6. 6Hold tobacco, right-click a crafting table to roll3 tobacco = 1 cigar
  7. 7Light it: flint and steel in your off hand, or a campfire, torch or lantern8 puffs
Cigar · Regeneration Gran Reserva · Regen + Resistance, finish it for Hero of the Village Honey · Absorption Chocolate · Haste Berry · Speed Glow · Night Vision Blaze · Fire Resistance

Puff three times fast and you cough. Lit cigars go out underwater.
